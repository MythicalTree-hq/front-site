AnomalyGuard

API anomaly detection for lotteries and online retailers. AnomalyGuard ingests millions of events a day, learns what normal looks like for each tenant, and raises the alarm the moment behaviour breaks pattern — per user and across your whole platform.

  • Real-time detection. Spike and multivariate models score every event as it arrives.
  • Per-tenant baselines. Your normal, not an industry average — thresholds scoped to each tenant and endpoint.
  • Alerts that explain themselves. Every alert carries its signals and reasons, ready for your team and your auditors.
  • Built for volume. Designed for ~1M events/day with spikes in the tens of thousands per 15 minutes.
